# DCA

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

This is short for Dollar Cost Averaging. A strategy where you invest a fixed amount of Bitcoin at regular intervals (daily, weekly, monthly) instead of trying to guess the best time to buy a large amount.

## Analogy

Instead of waiting for the "perfect" moment to buy Bitcoin at the lowest price (which you'll never know), you buy a little bit every week. Some weeks the price is high, some weeks it's low, but over time you end up with an average price that smooths out the market's ups and downs.

## In Context

*"I've been using DCA - buying 50 dollars of Bitcoin every Sunday for the past year."*

**What this means:** Every Sunday you buy the same dollar amount regardless of price. If the price drops, your 50 dollars buys more Bitcoin. If it rises, you get less. Over months and years, this averaging strategy removes emotion from timing decisions.

## Why It Matters

DCA is especially valuable for people earning regular income. It removes the need to time the market and is a disciplined way to build Bitcoin holdings over time. Most Bitcoin wealth has been built through consistent, smaller purchases over years.
