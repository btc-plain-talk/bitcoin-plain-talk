# Slippage

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

The difference between the price you expect to pay for Bitcoin and the price you actually pay when you execute the trade. High slippage means the price moved significantly between when you placed your order and when it was filled.

## Analogy

You're at the market and tell a trader "I'll buy Bitcoin at 45,000 naira." But by the time the trader executes your order, the price has jumped to 45,500 naira. You just experienced slippage, you ended up paying more than expected.

## In Context

*"I tried to sell Bitcoin on a low-liquidity exchange and experienced 2% slippage - I got less than I expected."*

**What this means:** The order book didn't have enough Bitcoin sellers at your desired price, so the exchange matched you with sellers at higher prices. This gap between expected and actual price is slippage.

## Why It Matters

Slippage matters more on smaller exchanges or when trading low-liquidity cryptocurrencies. Understanding slippage helps you make better trading decisions and avoid unexpected costs.
