# Finality

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

The point at which a Bitcoin transaction becomes so deeply embedded in the blockchain that reversing it is practically impossible.

## Analogy

Imagine writing your name in wet cement. When you first write it, someone could erase it. But after a few hours, the cement hardens and your mark is permanent. Bitcoin finality works similarly, after enough time and confirmations, a transaction becomes permanent.

## In Context

*"After six confirmations, a Bitcoin transaction has economic finality - the cost to reverse it exceeds any gain an attacker could make."*

**What this means:** While technically possible to reverse a transaction, doing so would require 51% of the network's computing power - an attack so expensive that it's economically irrational. This is why finality increases with each confirmation.

## Why It Matters

Finality is what makes Bitcoin trustworthy for large payments. Understanding finality helps you know when a payment is truly irreversible.
