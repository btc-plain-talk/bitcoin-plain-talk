# Double Spending

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

When someone tries to spend the same Bitcoin twice, sending it to two different people in two separate transactions. Bitcoin's network and blockchain prevent this from happening.

## Analogy

Imagine you have one naira note. You try to give it to your friend at the market, but you also try to give it to your mother at the same time. That's impossible, you can't be in two places at once, and the note can't be two places at once. Bitcoin solves this problem: the network checks that every Bitcoin is only spent once.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin solves the double-spending problem that digital currencies faced before it."*

**What this means:** Before Bitcoin, digital currencies were vulnerable to duplication attacks. Someone could copy a digital coin and send it to multiple people. Bitcoin's network prevents this by requiring consensus, every node agrees which transaction is valid first.

## Why It Matters

Double-spending protection is what makes Bitcoin money actually work. Without it, the currency would be worthless because anyone could fake infinite Bitcoin. This was the major innovation that made Bitcoin possible.
