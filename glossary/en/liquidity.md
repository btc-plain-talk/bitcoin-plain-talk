# Liquidity

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

How easily and quickly you can buy or sell Bitcoin without significantly moving the price, the availability of buyers and sellers at various prices.

## Analogy

Imagine trying to sell your car. If there are 100 people looking to buy cars at the market, you'll sell quickly at a good price (high liquidity). If only 2 people are interested, you might have to drop the price significantly (low liquidity).

## In Context

*"Bitcoin has high liquidity on Coinbase, you can sell millions of dollars instantly. On smaller exchanges, liquidity is lower."*

**What this means:** Major exchanges like Coinbase have thousands of active buyers and sellers, so you can sell any amount of Bitcoin quickly at fair prices. Small exchanges might have few buyers, so selling a large amount requires lowering the price.

## Why It Matters

Liquidity affects how much slippage you experience and how quickly you can enter or exit a position. High liquidity is good, it means tight bid-ask spreads and minimal price impact.
