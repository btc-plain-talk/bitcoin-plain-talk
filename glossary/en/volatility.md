# Volatility

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

How much and how quickly Bitcoin's price changes. High volatility means the price swings dramatically up and down. Low volatility means it changes slowly.

## Analogy

Imagine a weather forecast. Some days the temperature is stable, stays at 25°C all day, other days it jumps from 15°C to 30°C unpredictably. Bitcoin's volatility is like that, some weeks the price barely moves, other weeks it doubles or halves.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin's volatility has decreased over the years - early Bitcoin was much more volatile than it is now."*

**What this means:** When Bitcoin was new and had low adoption, small news could cause huge price swings. Now that it's worth trillions and traded globally, price movements are smaller in percentage terms.

## Why It Matters

Volatility affects how Bitcoin is used. High volatility makes it risky as a currency for everyday payments. Lower volatility makes Bitcoin more stable and usable. Volatility also creates opportunities and risks for traders.
