# Non Custodial Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A non custodial wallet is a Bitcoin wallet where YOU hold the secret keys, not a company. It means only you can touch your money, no one can freeze or take it.

## Analogy

Think of it like cash in your own pocket vs. cash given to a neighbor to keep for you. Custodial = you gave money to the neighbor, he can refuse to give it back. Non-custodial = money is in YOUR pocket, in your house, with your own key. You have full control.

## In Context

*"Don't keep your Bitcoin on an exchange, move it to a non-custodial wallet like  Manna ,Aqua or Muun."*

**What this means:** This means exchanges can be hacked, closed, or block your account. If you move your Bitcoin to your own non-custodial wallet, even if the exchange collapses, your money is still safe with you.


## Why It Matters

You should care because this is the whole point of Bitcoin - true freedom. With a non-custodial wallet, you become your own bank. Especially for us in Africa where banks can freeze accounts, this matters a lot. If you don't own the keys, it's not your Bitcoin.
