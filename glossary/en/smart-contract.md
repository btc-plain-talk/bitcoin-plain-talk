# Smart Contract

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A smart contract is a computer program stored on a blockchain that automatically carries out actions when specific conditions are met.

## Analogy

Imagine a locked savings box at a community meeting. Everyone agrees that the box will release money only when a particular condition is fulfilled. Nobody needs to convince the person holding the key because the agreed rule controls what happens.

A smart contract works similarly, except the rules are written in computer code and executed by the blockchain.

## In Context

*"The payment was released automatically after the smart contract confirmed that the conditions were met."*

**What this means:** The program checked whether the agreed requirements had been fulfilled and automatically carried out the payment.

## Why It Matters

Smart contracts allow people to create blockchain applications without relying entirely on a middleman to carry out every agreement. They are used in areas such as decentralized finance, digital marketplaces, and blockchain-based games.

However, smart contracts can contain coding mistakes. Once deployed, changing them may be difficult or impossible, depending on how they were designed.
