# miner

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

A miner is a person or computer that helps confirm Bitcoin transactions and keep the record safe, and in return, gets rewarded with new Bitcoin.

## Analogy

Think of a miner like a village accountant who sits with a big public notebook. Many people are sending money to each other. The accountant checks that no one is cheating, writes all the valid transactions in the notebook, locks the page, and gets paid a small fee for the hard work. Without him, the notebook would be messy and untrustworthy.

## In Context

*"Miners just confirmed my transaction, my Bitcoin arrived in 10 minutes."*

**What this means:** This means the network of miners has checked your transaction, agreed it is valid, and added it to the permanent record. That's why your money is now visible in the receiver's wallet.


## Why It Matters

You should care because miners are the reason Bitcoin works without a bank. They are the security guards. They make sure no one can double-spend or fake Bitcoin. No miners = no security = no trustworthy Bitcoin. Their work makes your savings safe.
