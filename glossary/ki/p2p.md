# P2P

**Category:** Community

## Plain English

Systems that work like an organized collective allowing each individual to interact directly with others ,so no bank is required as a third party.

## Analogy

Barter trade where two traders exchange desired items both deemed to be of equal value.

## In Context

*"My piece of land will be exchanged with 300 cows."*

**What this means:** If the land is worth X, the 300 cows value is deemed to be X.

## Why It Matters

This means that there no broker or intermediary to control price.

## Related Terms

- Markers
- takers
- "in order"
