# Decentralisation

**Olukongo:** Technology

## Plain English

Decentralisation ni engira ya Bitcoin ahu shibulaho omundu mulala, kampuni nohomba serikali ilamulanga. Amaelfu ka kompyuta khushialo shikholanga hamwenga okhuyimilisia system

## Olulinganisho

Benki ni linga tawa yimwe ya amatsi. Bitcoin ni linga emitsima mingi esaasaanie. Omunyala okhufunga emindi, halali shonyala okhumalisa chosi tawe.

## Mu Mbeele

*"Decentralisation ya Bitcoin ituma shiyinyala okhufungwa nende serikali. Kubanga network yikholanga mu shialo shingi, yikhwikalanga yikora"*

**Amakulu kwakwo:** In English, it means, Bitcoin’s decentralisation means that it cannot be shut down by the government. Because the network operates across many countries, it continues to function.

## Habwaki ni yákamaro

Decentralisation yikhola omusingi nende engira enkulu khwokhukhola kwa Bitcoin nende Web3.
