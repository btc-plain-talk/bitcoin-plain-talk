# Timelock

**Olukongo:** Technology

## Plain English

Timelock ni feature ya Bitcoin ibikanga Bitcoin mbu shishinyala okukoresibwa okhula khu ssaawa yatelekhanibwa nohomba block endala ya blockchain niyifwika.

## Olulinganisho

Linga safe ifungibwe mbu yikangulwe khu lunaku lwa mberi. Obikamo sente leero, mana oyifunga.
Shibulaho omundu, noba ni iwe mwene, unyala okhuyifungula okhula olwo lunaku lufike. Nikwo Timelock ikholanga khu Bitcoin transaction inyala okhukholwa leero, halali shiyinyala okukakasibwa okhula obwire obutelekhanibwe bufike.

## Mu Mbeele

*"Nakoresa Timelock okhutelekhania payment ya shinga wiiki ibili."*

**Amakulu kwakwo:** Wakhola transaction iloma mbu shiyinyala okukakasibwa okhula wiiki ibili shishile.
Omundu nagera okhuyituma mangu, network yikhayanga.

## Habwaki ni yákamaro

Timelock ni ya mana muno khu mirimo shinga Payment Channels (Lightning Network) nende Atomic Swaps. Kandi inyala okhukoresibwa okhutelekhania obusika nohomba payments shia mberi. Timelock yongera obunyali khu Bitcoin halali yikhwikalila obulindi.
