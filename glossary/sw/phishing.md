# Phishing

**Kategoria:** Usalama

## Maelezo Rahisi

Phishing ni time  scammer anajifanya kuwa kampuni enye unatrust, kama bank yako, M-Pesa au exchange, ili akuchukulie  credentials zako kama password au wallet, ama akuconvince umtumie doo.

## analogy

Tuseme umepata message ya M-Pesa inayosema, "Nimekutumia do by mistake, tafadhali nirudishie," tuseme ni 5,000. Message inaonekana legit na inakuharakisha, kama "tafadhali nisaidie haraka, nina mtu hospitali." Lakini anayekutumia ni stranger, hata humjui, na anataka pesa yako tu. Hivyo ndivyo phishing inavyofanya kazi. Scammers wanaiga wallet yako, exchange au bank unayoamini ili uwa-trust.

## Jinsi Inavyotumiwa

*Unapata email ama SMS inayosema account yangu ya exchange imefungwa ama iko na issue  na niclick link ili irekebishwe."*

**Maana yake:** Kwa percentage  kubwa hii ni phishing. Hiyo link mara nyingi inakupeleka kwa website fake inayofanana na ile real. Then Inaiba chochote utakachoweka ukiclick, kama credentials zako au private key yako. Kitu unafaa kufanya ni kuignore link, kisha ufungue app au website legit mwenyewe.

## Kwa Nini Ni Muhimu

Malipo ya Bitcoin hayawezi kurudishwa (reversed). Scammer akiiba coins zako kupitia phishing, mara nyingi ni ngumu kuzirudisha au kutrace. Hakuna wallet, exchange wala customer care atakayekuuliza seed phrase au private key yako, EVER. Kujua link fake, message za dharura kama "fanya hivi sasa," na offers zinakaa too good to be true ni njia bora ya kulinda coins zako.

## Maneno Yanayohusiana

- seedphrase
- private key
- Two-Factor Authentication
- exchange
