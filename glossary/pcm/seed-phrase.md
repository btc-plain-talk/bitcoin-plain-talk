# Seed Phrase

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na list of 12 or 24 ordinary words wey fit restore your entire Bitcoin wallet — na your ultimate backup if your device lost, stolen, or spoil.

## E Be Like

Think am like master key to your house wey you hide under stone for your compound. If you lose your regular keys (your device), you fit use the master key to enter. But if another person find that stone, dem fit walk in too. Your seed phrase dey work same way — whoever get am, get your bitcoin.

## How E Dey Used

*"Write down your seed phrase and keep am somewhere safe offline."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The person should write the 12 or 24 words with pen on paper (never take screenshot or type am online) and keep am somewhere secure — like inside safe or trusted place wey only dem know.

## Why E Matter

Your seed phrase na the only way to recover your wallet if something go wrong with your device. If you lose am, you don lose access to your bitcoin forever. But e also na the biggest security risk — if you store am carelessly, anybody wey find am fit take everything without leaving any trace.
