# Node

**Kain:** Network

## Simple Tori

Na computer wey dey keep full copy of Bitcoin blockchain and dey check say every transaction and block dey follow the rules — nodes na wetin dey enforce Bitcoin laws.

## E Be Like

Think of nodes like community elders for a village wey all of dem hold copy of the official record book. When somebody claim say transaction happen, all of dem go check their own copies. If even one record don tamper, the others go spot the fraud immediately. The more elders you get, the harder e dey for anybody to cheat.

## How E Dey Used

*"Running your own node mean you no need to trust anybody else."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Instead of depending on third-party service to tell you wetin dey on the blockchain, you dey verify everything yourself. Your node dey independently check say every transaction you receive dey valid and follow the rules.

## Why E Matter

Nodes na the backbone of Bitcoin decentralisation. No single node dey in charge. Each one dey independently enforce the same rules. This mean there no dey central point to attack, bribe, or shut down — e make Bitcoin resistant to censorship.
