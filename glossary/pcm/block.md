# Block

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na group of confirmed Bitcoin transactions wey dem bundle together and add permanently to the blockchain, roughly every 10 minutes.

## E Be Like

Think am like one page for community record book. Every 10 minutes, new page go complete, dem go stamp am and seal am inside the book forever. Each page (block) contain list of all the transactions wey happen for that time. Once dem seal am, nothing for that page fit change.

## How E Dey Used

*"Your transaction get 3 block confirmations."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Dem put the transaction inside one block, and 2 more blocks don add on top since then. Each new block make am harder and harder to reverse. After 6 confirmations, most services consider am final — e no go reverse.

## Why E Matter

Blocks na how Bitcoin reach agreement. Once transaction dey inside block — especially when several blocks don stack on top — e don settle permanently. No bank approval needed, no reversal possible.
