# Decentralisation

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na the property of Bitcoin wey mean no single person, company, or government dey in charge — thousands of independent computers all over the world dey maintain the system together.

## E Be Like

Traditional bank na like one water tank wey dey supply whole city. If dem damage or shut down the tank, nobody go get water. Bitcoin na like network of thousands of independent wells scattered across the country — you fit block some, but you can never stop all of dem.

## How E Dey Used

*"Bitcoin decentralisation make am resistant to government shutdown."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Because Bitcoin dey run on thousands of computers for different countries, no single authority fit turn am off. Even if government ban Bitcoin for one country, the network go continue running through nodes for every other country.

## Why E Matter

Decentralisation na wetin give Bitcoin its main property: nobody fit change the rules, freeze your funds, or shut am down. For people for countries with unstable governments or financial systems, this dey especially important — your money na your money, protected by maths, not by goodwill of any institution.
