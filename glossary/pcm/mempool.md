# Mempool

**Kain:** Network

## Simple Tori

Na waiting room for Bitcoin transactions wey never confirm yet — dem dey wait there before miner go pick dem up.

## E Be Like

Think say you wan send parcel through motor park. Before the bus leave, your parcel dey sit for the park waiting room until driver ready to go. Mempool na that waiting room — your transaction dey sit there until miner put am inside block.

## How E Dey Used

*"My transaction don stuck for mempool for one hour now."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The person send money but e never enter blockchain yet. E dey queue. If dem pay higher fee, e for don move front of the line.

## Why E Matter

When plenty people dey use Bitcoin at the same time, thousands of transactions go fill the mempool. Miners go pick the ones wey pay highest fee first. If you know how mempool work, you go sabi how much fee to pay and why your transaction fit take time.
