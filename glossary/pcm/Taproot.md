# Taproot

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na 2021 upgrade to Bitcoin wey dey make transactions more private, cheaper, and e enable more complex smart contracts, e no go dey visible for the blockchain.

## E Be Like

Before Taproot, every detail of transaction dey obvious for the blockchain, like everybody fit see inside your envelope before you send am. Taproot dey encrypt those details, so only the sender and receiver dey know wetin the transaction actually contain. To the rest of the network, e dey look like simple transaction.

## How E Dey Used

Taproot addresses dey start with 'bc1p', dem dey more private than regular addresses.

**Wetin dis mean:** When you send go Taproot address, the network dey know say transaction happen, but e no fit see the details. This dey make Bitcoin more private while e dey reduce transaction size and fees.

## Why E Matter

Taproot na one of Bitcoin most significant upgrades since SegWit. E improve privacy without needing extra space for the blockchain. E also enable more sophisticated Bitcoin contracts, like atomic swaps and complex payment channels, wey before time no dey possible or no dey efficient.
