# Proof of Work

**Kain:** Mining

## Simple Tori

Na the system Bitcoin dey use to make adding blocks to the blockchain expensive and difficult — computers must do real, costly computation to earn the right to record transactions.

## E Be Like

Imagine say to vote for community meeting, each person must first solve hard maths puzzle by hand. E go take effort and time. This one dey stop anybody from casting millions of fake votes — each one go require genuine work. Proof of Work na Bitcoin version of this: each block need provable computational effort before e go be accepted.

## How E Dey Used

*"Proof of Work make am economically impossible to rewrite Bitcoin history."*

**Wetin dis mean:** To change past transaction on the blockchain, attacker go need to redo all the computational work for that block AND every block wey dem add since — while at the same time outpacing the entire honest network. The energy cost make am practically impossible.

## Why E Matter

Proof of Work na wetin make Bitcoin trustworthy without any central authority. You no need to trust any person or company — you dey trust the maths and the energy cost. To change the rules go require overpowering all the mining hardware for the whole world.
