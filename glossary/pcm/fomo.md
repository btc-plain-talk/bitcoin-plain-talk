# FOMO

**Kain:** Community

## Simple Tori

FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out), is the anxious feeling that makes people rush to buy/invest in Bitcoin during a price surge because they fear missing out on potential gains if they wait.

## E Be Like

E be like when everybody rush go buy something because dem dey fear say price go soon go up, so you sef rush buy am before e go cost pass how e be.

## How E Dey Used

*"Omo Bitcoin don dey go up o, I no wan dey behind abeg, make I rush buy small now before e go cost pass like this.."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Wetin e mean be say, the person wan rush buy Bitcoin because dem dey fear say the price go just dey go up like that and dem no wan miss out wen people begin cash out their gain.

## Why E Matter

As person wey just start to do Bitcoin, you go need to know wetin FOMO mean because e go help you try avoid to dey buy Bitcoin because say everybody dey buy am, instead e go help you calm down to make better decision than say you go dey rush rush buy because of fear.

## Wordings Wey Relate

- FUD
- Market Sentiment
- Bull Market
- HODL etc
