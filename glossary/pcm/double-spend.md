# Double spending

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

When somebody try to spend the same Bitcoin twice - sending am to two different people for two separate transactions. Bitcoin network and blockchain dey prevent this from happening.

## E Be Like

Imagine say you get one naira note. You try to give am to your friend for the market, but you also try to give am to your mother at the same time. That dey impossible, you no fit be for two places at once, and the note no fit be for two places at once. Bitcoin dey solve this problem: the network dey check say every Bitcoin only dey spend once.

## How E Dey Used

*"Bitcoin dey solve the double-spending problem wey digital currencies face before am."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Before Bitcoin, digital currencies dey vulnerable to duplication attacks. Somebody fit copy digital coin and send am to multiple people. Bitcoin network dey prevent this by requiring consensus, every node dey agree which transaction dey valid first.

## Why E Matter

Double-spending protection na wetin dey make Bitcoin money actually work. Without am, the currency go dey worthless because anybody fit fake infinite Bitcoin. This na the major innovation wey make Bitcoin possible - e dey prove say digital scarcity dey achievable without central authority.
