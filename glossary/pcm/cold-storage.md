# Cold Storage

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na keeping your Bitcoin private keys on device wey never connect to internet — na the safest way to store bitcoin wey you no plan to spend soon.

## E Be Like

Think am like burying cash inside waterproof container for your backyard versus keeping am for your pocket. Wetin you bury dey harder to steal because thief go need to physically know where to dig. Cold storage mean your keys never dey online, so hackers get no way to reach dem from far.

## How E Dey Used

*"I keep most of my bitcoin for cold storage and only small amount for hot wallet for daily use."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The person dey store the bulk of their savings on hardware device or offline paper wallet wey never connect to internet. Dem only keep small amount for app on their phone for easy access when dem need am.

## Why E Matter

The biggest risk to bitcoin savings na online theft. Cold storage dey remove that risk completely — if your keys never dey online, hackers no fit steal dem online. For anybody wey dey hold significant amounts, cold storage dey considered as essential security.
