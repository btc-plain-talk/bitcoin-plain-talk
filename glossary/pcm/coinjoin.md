# Coinjoin

**Kain:** Privacy

## Simple Tori

Na method where multiple people dey combine their Bitcoin transactions together to increase privacy, making am harder to track who send money to who.

## E Be Like

Imagine say ten people dey market, each with money to send to different person. Instead of sending individually (where everybody fit see who dey pay who), dem dey put all their money for pot, mix am up, and then distribute am to the destinations. Now nobody fit tell which source pay which destination.

## How E Dey Used

I dey use Coinjoin to increase the privacy of my transaction.

**Wetin dis mean:** You send your Bitcoin through service wey dey combine am with other people Bitcoin for single transaction, then e dey distribute the outputs. One outside observer fit see say transaction happen, but dem no fit determine which input correspond to which output.

## Why E Matter

Bitcoin dey pseudonymous but no dey anonymous, transactions dey visible for the blockchain.  Person wey dey determined enough fit track your spending. Coinjoin dey make tracking much harder. Privacy advocates dey use Coinjoin to reclaim financial privacy, but be aware, some exchanges and services dey look down on CoinJoin because e dey increase suspicion.
