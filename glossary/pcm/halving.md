# Halving

**Kain:** Economy

## Simple Tori

Na event wey dey happen roughly every four years where the reward miners collect for adding new block go cut to half — e dey permanently reduce how fast new bitcoin dey enter circulation.

## E Be Like

Imagine gold mine wey dey produce 50 nuggets per day. After four years, e suddenly start to produce only 25. Four years later, 12.5. E dey keep halving until the mine nearly empty. Bitcoin dey work same way — the "mine" get fixed schedule wey dem put inside the code, and everybody for the world fit see exactly when the next halving go happen.

## How E Dey Used

*"The 2024 halving reduce block reward from 6.25 to 3.125 bitcoin."*

**Wetin dis mean:** From April 2024, miners now dey collect 3.125 bitcoin for every block dem mine, instead of 6.25. This mean new bitcoin dey create at exactly half the previous rate, making am more scarce over time.

## Why E Matter

Halvings na how Bitcoin enforce its fixed supply of 21 million coins. By reducing issuance through the code, the system make sure nobody fit inflate bitcoin supply — unlike government wey fit print more money. Historically, halvings don come before serious price increase as supply reduce while demand dey grow.
