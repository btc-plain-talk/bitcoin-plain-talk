# Satoshi

**Kain:** Economy

## Simple Tori

Na the smallest unit of bitcoin — one bitcoin equal 100 million satoshi, so e possible to own or send very small fraction of bitcoin.

## E Be Like

Satoshi na to bitcoin as kobo na to naira — except the fractions dey even smaller. If one bitcoin cost plenty money, satoshi allow you deal with small, affordable amounts. You no need to buy whole bitcoin any more than you need to buy whole kilogram of gold.

## How E Dey Used

*"I send 5,000 satoshi as tip."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The person send 0.00005 bitcoin — very small amount, good for tip or micropayment. At any bitcoin price, satoshi make am easy to send exact small values.

## Why E Matter

Many people think say dem no fit afford bitcoin because the price per coin dey look too high. Satoshi remove that barrier — you fit buy, save, and send any amount, no matter how small. Na how bitcoin become accessible for everybody.
