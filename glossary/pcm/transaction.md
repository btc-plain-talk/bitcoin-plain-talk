# Transaction

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na record of Bitcoin wey somebody dey send from one wallet go another one. Every transaction dey broadcast to the network, nodes dey verify am, and e dey store permanent for the blockchain.

## E Be Like

Think of receipt from market. When you buy something, you get small paper wey dey say who pay wetin, when, and where. Bitcoin transaction na the same, na the proof say somebody send Bitcoin to somebody else for specific time.

## How E Dey Used

My transaction get three confirmations.

**Wetin dis mean:** The transaction wey you send don dey recorded for three separate blocks on the blockchain. The more confirmations wey e get, the more permanent and irreversible that transaction go be.

## Why E Matter

Transactions na wetin Bitcoin dey build for, the ability to move value from one person to another one. Every transaction dey transparent, once e confirm, e dey irreversible, and nobody fit censor am. Once transaction get enough confirmations, e dey final.
