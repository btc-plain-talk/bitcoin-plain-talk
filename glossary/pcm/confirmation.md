# Confirmation

**Kain:** Transactions

## Simple Tori

Confirmation dey happen each time new block add to the blockchain on top of the block wey contain your transaction — more confirmations mean your payment don settle and e no go reverse.

## E Be Like

Imagine fresh concrete on road. Right after dem pour am, e still soft — person fit still disturb am. After one hour e dey start to harden. After one day e solid. Bitcoin confirmations dey work same way: each new block wey dem add on top of yours na another layer of concrete wey dey harden the transaction into permanence.

## How E Dey Used

*"Wait for at least 6 confirmations before you release the goods."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The seller want to see 6 blocks stacked on top of the payment transaction before dem trust say e don final. Six confirmations mean roughly 60 minutes don pass and the cost to reverse the transaction don become astronomically high.

## Why E Matter

To know how confirmations dey work help you understand when payment truly final. For small everyday purchases, 1-2 confirmations fit be enough. For large transfers, to wait for 6 or more na safer and na the standard wey most exchanges and services dey use.
