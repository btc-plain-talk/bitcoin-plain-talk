# Whitepaper

**Kain:** Community

## Simple Tori

A white paper is a document that explains the idea behind Bitcoin, how it works, and how people can send money directly to each other without relying on banks.

## E Be Like

E be like when person get idea to start something new, e go first write down wetin the thing go do, how e go work, and the problem wey e wan solve, so other people fit understand am before dem start to use am.

## How E Dey Used

*"If you wan understand why dem create Bitcoin and how e dey work, try read the Bitcoin white paper wey Satoshi Nakamoto write."*

**Wetin dis mean:** E mean say if you wan really understand the idea behind Bitcoin, why dem create am, and how e dey work without banks, you fit read the original document wey Satoshi Nakamoto write wey him use explain everything.

## Why E Matter

E important because Bitcoin white paper go help you understand why dem create Bitcoin, the problem wey e wan solve, and how people fit send money to each other without depending on banks. E go also help you separate wetin Bitcoin really be from all the rumours and wrong information wey people dey spread about am.
