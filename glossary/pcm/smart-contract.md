# Smart Contract

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Smart contract na computer program wey dey blockchain and fit carry out action automatically when certain conditions don meet.

## E Be Like

Imagine say people gather for community meeting and agree say dem go keep money inside one box. Dem agree say the box go only release the money when one particular condition don happen.

Nobody need to beg the person wey dey hold the key because na the agreement determine wetin go happen.

Smart contract dey work like that, but instead of person dey enforce the agreement, computer code dey follow the rules for blockchain.

## How E Dey Used

*"The payment don release automatically after the smart contract confirm say all the conditions don meet."*

**Wetin dis mean:** The program check whether everything wey dem agree on don happen, then e automatically carry out the payment.

## Why E Matter

Smart contracts allow people to build blockchain applications without depending completely on middlemen to carry out every agreement. Dem dey useful for things like decentralized finance, digital marketplaces, and blockchain games.

But smart contracts fit get coding mistakes. After dem don deploy am, e fit hard or even impossible to change am, depending on how dem design am.
