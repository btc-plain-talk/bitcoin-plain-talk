# Mining

**Kain:** Mining

## Simple Tori

Na the process where powerful computers dey compete to solve puzzle, and the winner go add the next block of transactions to the blockchain and collect newly created bitcoin as reward.

## E Be Like

Imagine competition where thousands of people dey each roll big set of dice, racing to be the first to roll one specific combination. The winner go write the next page for community record book and dem go pay am for doing so. Bitcoin mining na that competition — except say computers dey roll the dice billions of times per second.

## How E Dey Used

*"Mining bitcoin for Africa dey become more viable with cheap solar energy."*

**Wetin dis mean:** As the cost of electricity (the main cost of mining) dey drop — for example through solar panels — e dey become more profitable to run the computers wey dey secure Bitcoin network and earn mining rewards.

## Why E Matter

Mining na wetin make Bitcoin secure without any central authority. No company or government dey add new blocks — computers all over the world dey compete fairly to do am. Miners also na how new bitcoin dey enter circulation, following fixed schedule wey go end at 21 million coins.
