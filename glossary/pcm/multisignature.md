# Multi-signature

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na wallet wey dey need multiple private keys to approve one transaction, instead of just one. People dey write am as "multisig."

## E Be Like

Think of safe for bank wey dey need three keys to open. Even if somebody thief one key, dem still no fit open the safe without the other two. Multi-signature dey work the same way, you fit set am up so three out of five people must approve transaction before e dey go through.

## How E Dey Used

We set up 2-of-3 multisig wallet for our Bitcoin savings.

**Wetin dis mean:** The wallet go need signatures from 2 out of 3 owners before any Bitcoin fit leave. If na one person key dem steal, the funds still dey safe because the thief need at least one other owner approval.

## Why E Matter

Multi-signature na one of the best security practices for Bitcoin. E dey protect against theft (no single point of failure) and prevent accident mistakes (require consensus). Families, companies, and groups dey often use multisig to secure large amounts of Bitcoin.
