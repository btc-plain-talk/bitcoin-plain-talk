# BIP

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na short for Bitcoin Improvement Proposal. Na formal document wey dey describe suggested upgrade or change to Bitcoin rules or standards.

## E Be Like

Think of BIP as petition to improve your city. Somebody dey write down idea for improvement, present am to community, discuss am, and if enough people agree, dem dey implement am. Bitcoin improvement proposals dey work the same way, anybody fit write one, but e go only become reality if the community (miners, developers, nodes) accept am.

## How E Dey Used

Them introduce SegWit as BIP 141.

**Wetin dis mean:** Different upgrades dey get different BIP numbers. BIP 141 na the official proposal for SegWit, BIP 32 na for hierarchical deterministic wallets, etc. Developers and miners dey reference BIPs to understand wetin change people dey discuss.

## Why E Matter

BIPs na how Bitcoin dey evolve without central authority. Anybody fit propose one, but adoption dey voluntary. This one dey keep Bitcoin decentralised, no single person fit force change on entire network. The BIP process dey transparent and community-driven.
