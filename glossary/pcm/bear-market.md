# Bear Market

**Kain:** Community

## Simple Tori

A Bear Market is when Bitcoin prices keep falling over time, and many people expect them to drop even further.

## E Be Like

Na like when people wey dey sell phone begin reduce their prices because buyers no dey come, and everybody dey expect say the price go drop more.

## How E Dey Used

*"Bitcoin dey enter bear market, price don dey fall for some time now, and e be like say e fit still drop more."*

**Wetin dis mean:** E mean say Bitcoin price don dey fall for some time now, and people dey expect say e fit continue to drop, so dem dey careful about buying.

## Why E Matter

E help you understand say Bitcoin price fit continue to fall for a long time, and you no need to panic or rush sell because of fear. E also teach you to invest only wetin you fit afford to leave for a while, because the market no dey always go up.
