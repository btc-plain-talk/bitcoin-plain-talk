# Altcoin

**Kain:** Community

## Simple Tori

Na any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin. The term literally mean "alternative coin".

## E Be Like

If Bitcoin na dollars, altcoins na other currencies like euros, yen, or Nigerian naira. Some fit be more useful for specific purposes, some fit be scams, but dem all dey alternatives to Bitcoin.

## How E Dey Used

Ethereum and Litecoin na popular altcoins.

**Wetin dis mean:** Bitcoin na the first cryptocurrency. Every other coin, Ethereum, Monero, Litecoin, Dogecoin, etc, people dey call them altcoin. Some altcoins get real use cases, while others dey purely speculative or scams.

## Why E Matter

Understanding altcoins dey important because the crypto market dey full of them. While Bitcoin dey focus on being sound money, some altcoins dey aim to do other things, run programs, enable privacy, serve specific communities. But dey careful: many altcoins na scams design to take your money. Bitcoin dey remain the most secure, most decentralise, and most trust cryptocurrency.
