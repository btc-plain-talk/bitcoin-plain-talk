# Inflation

**Kain:** Economy

## Simple Tori

Inflation is when the prices of everyday goods and services rise over time, making your money buy less than it used to.

## E Be Like

Make I use Nigeria as example
E be like like when #1,000 fit buy one family loaf before, but now, that same #1,000 no fit even buy half of that bread because say everything don cost.

## How E Dey Used

*"Inflation don make everything cost for this Nigeria, imagine #5,000 wey person fit use buy plenty things for supermarket before no fit even buy 2 packets of milo now. "*

**Wetin dis mean:** Wetin e mean be say prices of things don too cost, and na the same amount of money wey person fit use buy plenty things for supermarket before but now e no fit buy as e dey buy again because money don lose power.

## Why E Matter

E dey important because inflation dey reduce wetin your money fit buy as time dey go. Person wey understand wetin inflation dey talk e go help the person know why some people dey choose Bitcoin to use guide their savings, as per say Bitcoin get limited supply. But remember say Bitcoin price fit still rise or fall, so e no guarantee say your money go always maintain value.
