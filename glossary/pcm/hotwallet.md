# Hot Wallet

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na Bitcoin wallet wey dey connect to the internet, design for frequent spending and convenience instead of maximum security.

## E Be Like

Hot wallet na like when you get money for your pocket, e dey accessible and easy to spend, but person still fit thief am. Cold wallet na like when you lock money for safe for house. Most people dey use both: keep some money for pocket for daily use, and the rest for house for safekeeping.

## How E Dey Used

I dey keep small amount of Bitcoin for hot wallet for my phone for daily purchases.

**Wetin dis mean:** You dey store Bitcoin for wallet wey connect to the internet, making am quick to send from anywhere. But because e dey online, e get more security risks than offline wallets.

## Why E Matter

Hot wallets dey necessary for active Bitcoin users. You need one to buy things and receive payments. But security experts dey recommend say you only keep amount you need for short-term use for hot wallet, and store the rest for cold storage. This dey balance convenience with security.
