# Finality

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

The point where Bitcoin transaction become so deep embed for the blockchain say reversing am dey practically impossible.

## Analogy

Imagine say you write your name for wet cement. When you first write am, somebody fit erase am. But after few hours, the cement go hard and your mark go dey permanent. Bitcoin finality dey work the same way - after enough time and confirmations, transaction dey become permanent.

## In Context

*"After six confirmations, Bitcoin transaction get economic finality - the cost to reverse am dey exceed any gain one attacker fit make."*

**What this means:** While e dey technically possible to reverse transaction, to do am go require 51% of the network computing power - attack dey so expensive say e dey economically irrational. That na why finality dey increase with each confirmation.

## Why It Matters

Finality na wetin dey make Bitcoin trustworthy for large payments. One merchant no go hand over goods for transaction wey only dey 1 block deep. But after 6 blocks (roughly one hour), the transaction dey so final say the risk of reversal dey negligible. Understanding finality dey help you know when payment truly dey irreversible.
