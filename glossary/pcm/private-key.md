# Private Key

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na secret code wey prove say you own your bitcoin and allow you to spend am — anybody wey hold this key dey control the money.

## E Be Like

Think am like your bank PIN, except say no bank dey to call if you forget am, and nobody fit reset am for you. If another person get your private key, dem fit take everything. If you lose am, you don lose access to your bitcoin — forever.

## How E Dey Used

*"Never share your private key with anybody."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Your private key na the master password to your bitcoin. To share am na like you give person your whole wallet and say "take wetin you want." No legit service go ever ask you for am.

## Why E Matter

Bitcoin no get customer service. No bank to call, no fraud department to report to. Private key na the only proof of ownership. To keep am secret and safe na the most important thing in Bitcoin security.
