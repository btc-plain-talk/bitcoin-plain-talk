# Change Address

**Kain:** Technology

## Simple Tori

Na new address wey dey receive the leftover Bitcoin from transaction. E dey similar to when you collect change for market when you pay with more than the exact amount.

## E Be Like

You go market, go buy something for 500 naira but na only 1000 naira note you get. The seller con  give you 500 naira back as change. Bitcoin dey work similarly, if you send 2 Bitcoin but you only need to spend 1.5, the remaining 0.5 go to change address (usually one you dey control).

## How E Dey Used

*"When I send 1 Bitcoin, my wallet dey automatically generate change address for the 0.3 Bitcoin I no spend."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Your transaction get input of 1 BTC and output of 0.7 BTC to the recipient. The remaining 0.3 BTC go to change address wey your wallet dey control. If you reuse the same address, this change dey leak information about your balance.

## Why E Matter

Understanding change addresses dey important for privacy. Every time you spend Bitcoin, change address dey create. If you reuse addresses, outside observer fit track how much Bitcoin dey remain for your wallet. The best practice na to use new change address for each transaction.
