# Hardware Wallet

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na physical device, usually e dey look like USB drive, wey dey store your Bitcoin private keys offline and dey sign transactions securely.

## E Be Like

Hardware wallet na like secure briefcase for your Bitcoin. You dey keep your money inside, and whenever you wan spend am, you dey unlock the case with password. The money dey seal inside, even if thief steal the briefcase, dem no fit get the money without the password.

## How E Dey Used

I use hardware wallet like Ledger or Trezor to store my Bitcoin.

**Wetin dis mean:** Your private keys dey store for special device wey no dey connect to the internet. When you wan spend Bitcoin, you go connect the device to computer, approve the transaction for the device itself, and then e go broadcast the sign transaction to the network. Your keys no dey ever touch the internet.

## Why E Matter

Hardware wallets na the ideal balance between security and convenience. Dem dey much safer than hot wallets because your private keys no dey ever touch one internet-connect device, but dem dey more convenient than paper wallets because you fit easily spend Bitcoin. Most serious Bitcoin users dey keep their long-term savings for hardware wallet.
