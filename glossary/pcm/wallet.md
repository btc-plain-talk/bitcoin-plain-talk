# Wallet

**Kain:** Security

## Simple Tori

Na app or device wey dey hold your Bitcoin keys and allow you to send and receive bitcoin — but e no dey store coins, e dey store the keys wey prove say you own dem.

## E Be Like

Bitcoin wallet be like keychain, not like the wallet wey you put for your pocket. Your bitcoin dey for the blockchain; the wallet dey hold the keys wey go open am. If you lose your keychain, e no mean the house disappear — but without the keys, you no fit enter.

## How E Dey Used

*"I move my bitcoin comot from exchange put for my own wallet."*

**Wetin dis mean:** Instead of leaving bitcoin under company control (like bank), the person move am to wallet where only dem get the keys. This mean only dem fit access or send those funds.

## Why E Matter

If you keep bitcoin for exchange, na them you dey trust. If dem get hacked, go bankrupt, or freeze your account, you go lose access. When you hold your own wallet, the saying is "not your keys, not your coins" — but with that power, you also carry the responsibility to keep your keys safe.
